# Cross-Searcher Completeness Gate

**Status:** Open
**Type:** Backlog
**Source:** Design discussion

## Summary
Nothing confirms the whole report's combined checklist is covered across all tasks.

## Detail
While individual Searchers may complete their assigned tasks, there's no gate that verifies:
1. All checklist items across all tasks are addressed
2. No gaps exist between different task coverages
3. The combined output covers the full scope

This is essentially Phase 2's responsibility but needs explicit validation.
